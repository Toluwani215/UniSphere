# UniSphere

A multi-university digital platform for verified university announcements and student services
UniSphere is a proposed multi-university digital platform designed to make important campus information easier for students to access, understand and manage. 
## Project Overview

The goal of UniSphere is to connect students with reliable university information through one organised digital platform.
The project will initially focus on Veritas University, Abuja, with the possibility of expanding to other universities in the future. 

## Proposed Features 

-**Campus Announcements:** Organised university-wide updates.
-**CourseConnect:** Important announcements organised by individual courses.
-**Smart Notifications:** Alerts for important academic updates. 
-**Announcement Archive:** Search and access previous announcements.
-**Role-Based Access :** Different permissions for students, lecturers and authorised representatives.

## Initial Institution 

Veritas University, Abuja, Nigeria.

## Development Status 

Currently in the research and planning phase.

##Future Vision 

UniSphere aims to grow into a multi-university platform that improves how students access important academic and campus information.
