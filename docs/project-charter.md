# UniSphere - Project charter 

##1. Project Overview 

UniSphere is a multi-university digital platform designed to help students access important and verified campus information in one organised place.

##2. Project Vision 

To connect students with reliable campus information and essential academic updates through one accessible platform. 

##3. Problem Statement

Important academic announcements are often shared across different communication channels. Students may struggle to locate important information or find previous announcements.

##4. Proposed Solution 

UniSphere will provide a centralised platform where students access important university, faculty, departmental and course announcements.

##5. Main Objectives 

-Provide organised university announcements.
-Allow authorised lecturers and representatives to publish updates.
-Help students access information relevant to their courses.
-Provide notifications for important announcements.
-Make previous announcements searchable.

##6. Initial Target Institution 

Veritas University, Abuja, Nigeria.

##7. Future Expansion

The platform is designed with the possibility of expanding to other universities.

##8. Initial Development Priority 

Verified campus announcements and course-related information. 

##9. Project Status

Research and planning
